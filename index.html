Multi-channel campaign: lower cost per lead across Meta, Google and DSP

  • Performance marketing
  • Budget allocation
  • Meta Ads
  • Google Ads

Problem

Lead cost was rising while budget was split evenly across channels with no performance logic.

My role

I owned strategy, budget decisions and weekly optimization. An agency handled day-to-day execution.

Decisions

  • Moved budget from broad display to the two best-converting audiences
  • Paused creatives that fell below a set conversion threshold
  • Added a weekly review with a fixed KPI sheet

Result (sample figures)

Cost per lead, before100
Cost per lead, after62

Lead volume up 41% on the same budget.

Reporting: one dashboard for three brands

  • Business intelligence
  • Power BI
  • Social analytics

Problem

Social performance reports were built by hand from separate exports, so they arrived late and leadership questioned the numbers.

My role

I designed the data model, the dashboard and the monthly review process.

Decisions

  • Standardized metric definitions across all brands
  • Replaced manual copy-paste with a single combined tracker
  • Added a view for content type, so the team could see what to make more of

  • Marketing automation
  • n8n (Docker)
